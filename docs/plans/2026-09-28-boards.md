# Boards: the first build

> **Decisions:** `docs/16-Desk.md` §3.1 (*2026-09-28*). **Replaces:** 14 §4.2.
> **Contract:** `electron/src/shared/board.ts` (types for items, assets, the anchor, links); Hester mirrors it.
> **Builds on:** the Desk store (`hester/daemon/cockpit/desk.py`), the Desk surface (`electron/src/renderer/components/desk/DeskSurface.tsx`) and the Page's Asks and hand-offs (`hester/daemon/cockpit/deep.py`, `deep_ask.py`).

## 1. Scope

In: a `board` card kind; images, annotations (text boxes, optionally pinned), highlights, freehand lines; Ask and hand-off cards on the canvas from a selection; `source` on every asset; `[[` links between Boards and Pages; the Desk card's preview; `hester desk board` for Claude.

Out (later): the Visualize hand-off, Renders (14 §9), refresh from source, Boards on devices beyond the preview picture, shapes and connectors.

## 2. Store (Hester)

```
.hester/desk/boards/<bd-id>/
  card.json        {id, kind: 'board', title, origin, created_at, updated_at, last_touched_at}
  board.json       {version, items: [...]}          whole document, versioned like page.md (1 MB cap)
  answers.jsonl    Asks and hand-offs (deep.py's rows; anchor kind 'board')
  assets.jsonl     one row per asset: {name, mime, bytes, created_at, source?}
  assets/          img-<hex>.png|jpg (images), sel-<hex>.png (flattened selections)
  preview.png      the Board as a picture, written by Lee
```

Ids are `bd-<hex>`. In `desk.json` a Board is a card entry with `kind: 'board'`, so Areas, Move, Stash, Delete and the Drawer need nothing new.

**Items** (all have `id`, `x`, `y`, `w`, `h`, `z` in Board coordinates):

| kind | fields |
|---|---|
| `image` | `asset` (name in `assets/`) |
| `note` (annotation) | `text` (markdown, `[[` links allowed), `pin?: {item_id, u, v}` (0–1 within the item) |
| `highlight` | `item_id?` (the image it's on) |
| `stroke` | `points`, `width` |
| `ask` | `answer_id`, `target: {item_ids, rect}` |
| `handoff` | `answer_id`, `target: {item_ids, rect}` |
| `link` | `card_id` |

**Asset source** (optional, on `assets.jsonl` rows, and on Page assets too): `{kind: 'card', card_id, item_id?} | {kind: 'answer', card_id, answer_id} | {kind: 'file', path} | {kind: 'url', url}`, plus `taken_at`. Recorded only; nothing refreshes yet.

**Anchor** for a Board's Ask or hand-off: `{kind: 'board', item_ids, rect, snapshot: 'assets/sel-<hex>.png', notes: [annotation text]}`.

## 3. Routes (Hester :9000)

- `POST /desk/boards {area_id?, title?, x?, y?}`; `GET|PATCH|DELETE /desk/boards/{id}` (delete takes `{force}` like a Page)
- `GET /desk/boards/{id}/board`, `PUT /desk/boards/{id}/board {version, items}` (409 on a stale version)
- `POST /desk/boards/{id}/assets` (raw PNG or JPEG ≤ 10 MB, `?source=` JSON), `GET /desk/boards/{id}/assets/{name}`, `GET /desk/boards/{id}/assets` (the rows)
- `PUT /desk/boards/{id}/preview`, `GET /desk/boards/{id}/preview`
- `GET /desk/boards/{id}/answers`, `POST …/asks`, `POST …/handoffs`, `PATCH|retry …/answers/{aid}`: the Page's routes over the Board's store
- `GET /desk` lists Boards like Pages (`kind: 'board'`); `hester desk overview` shows them; `hester desk board <id>` prints the items (annotation text, highlights, links), the asks and hand-offs, and the asset paths

**Asks with an image:** `deep_ask` sends the snapshot as an image part through a cloud model that reads images (Gemini, the voice provider's key); a Board Ask never routes local. If no such model is configured the Ask fails with a clear message.

## 4. Lee (renderer)

- **Shared canvas code:** lift the camera, gestures, stroke drawing and undo out of `DeskSurface` into `lib/canvas/` so the Desk and the Board share them.
- **`components/board/BoardSurface.tsx`**, the zoomed Board: its own taskbar with **Select** (`V`: click selects, drag moves, handles resize, drag on bare canvas marquees; Space-drag or two fingers pan), **Annotate** (`A`: click drops a text box; clicking on an image or highlight pins it), **Highlight** (`H`: drag a region on an image), **Draw** (`D`). `⌘V` / drop add images at the cursor; `Delete`, `⌘Z` / `⇧⌘Z`; `Esc` back to Select, then out to the Desk.
- **Saving:** `board.json` is PUT debounced (as the Page is) with its version; `preview.png` is redrawn after a save, at most every few seconds.
- **Ask / Hand off on a selection** (`components/board/BoardView.tsx`): `⌘.` puts the keyboard in the same action row as a Page (Ask, Hand off). Lee flattens the selection to a PNG (an offscreen canvas of the images plus markup), uploads it, then posts the Ask or hand-off with the `board` anchor and places a sticky or clipboard item beside the selection. Clicking one expands it in place: the question and answer (with follow-up), or the brief, state and result.
- **Desk:** a `board` card draws `preview.png` (fetched into a blob URL, as Page images are); "New Board here" in the Area menu; pasting an image on an empty part of an Area in Cursor starts a Board with it.
- **Links:** the Page's `[[` picker lists Desk cards (Pages and Boards) above files and inserts `[[bd-…|Title]]` / `[[pg-…|Title]]`; clicking one zooms to that card. A Board's link item and annotation text use the same form.
- **Send to Lee:** with a Board zoomed, a device's images land as image items at the centre of the view, `source` `{kind: 'url'|'file'}` if the device gave one.

## 5b. Next round: B5 and B6 (*built 2026-09-28*)

**B5 Boards in sessions and on devices.**
- A zoomed Board is the session's card like a Page: widen the `pg-`-only places to `bd-` (card_kind `board`): `cockpitMode` (`isPageId` in logZoom, rememberLast, startOnCard, zoomIntoCard's touched list, sessionCard), main's `focus.ts` parseCardId, `queue.ts`'s card_kind check, `tabs-main.ts` deepRef. So a Board is `/desk/last`, on the touched list, and Deep reopens on it.
- **Send to Lee into a Board:** `/tether/targets` offers `{kind: 'board', card_id, title}` (the focus when a Board is zoomed, and Boards touched this session); main validates it like a Page (never `submit`); the renderer's sink puts images as image items in the middle of the view (source `{kind: 'url'|'file'}` only if the device gave one) and text as a note.
- **Review on the phone:** `GET /tether/desk` cards carry `kind`; `GET /tether/boards/:id` → `TetherBoard` (`shared/tether.ts`) and `GET /tether/boards/:id/preview` (PNG). Aeronaut's Review opens a Board as its picture (pinch to zoom) with its notes, links, asks and hand-offs below. The T-Deck stays Pages only.
- **End session on a Board** fills the ritual from the Board (its notes and open asks) as it does from a Page.
- **Voice hints:** `voice/hints` gives a Board's title and note text as terms.

**B6 Visualize.** Hester already has the pieces: the `diagram` agent (`registries/agents.yaml`, prompt `registries/prompts/visualize.md`) with `render_mermaid`, `generate_image` (Gemini image) and `render_markdown` (`tools/visualization_tools.py`). Visualize runs that agent, not a Claude task:
- `POST /desk/boards/{id}/visualize` `VisualizeCreate {brief, anchor}` → an answers.jsonl row `{kind: 'visualize', …, visual: null}`, run in the background like an Ask, given the selection's picture, its notes and the brief. When done, `visual` is a `VisualResult`: an image is saved as a Board asset with source `{kind: 'answer', card_id, answer_id}`; a Mermaid diagram or markdown is kept as text. Errors end in `error`; Retry works; deep:answer events as for Asks. `generate_image` reads the key the way the rest of Hester does (`voice/config.google_api_key`), not only `GOOGLE_API_KEY`.
- In Lee, the action row on a Board selection gains **Visualize** (`v`): a brief field, then a `visual` item (`BoardVisual`) beside the selection that shows progress; when done, Lee adds the result beside it once (an image item; a Mermaid diagram rendered to PNG in the renderer and uploaded with the same answer source; markdown as a note) and sets `result_item_id`.
- On a Page, Visualize comes later (a Page can't embed; it would go on a Board).
